# RouterGovernanceFixture

[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/cb1d9cc5d144b8c5c8b47688dd34b616792f0d5e/contracts/test/RouterGovernance.t.sol)

**Inherits:** [SafeGovernance](/contracts/test/helpers/abstract.SafeGovernance)

<i>

The weight-setter topology stage 7 and stage 11 build: the weight setter
holds the router's ADMIN_ROLE and WEIGHT_SETTER_ROLE and the register's
ADMIN_ROLE, and points at the register. `govAdmin` is who holds the
weight setter's ADMIN_ROLE (the deployer before stage 11, the timelock
after it).

</i>
