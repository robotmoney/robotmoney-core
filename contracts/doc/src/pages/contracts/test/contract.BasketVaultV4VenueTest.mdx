### test_manipulation_flashSwapPokeDepositRevertsOnTheDeviationGuard

A flash swap that moves spot, then a poke, then a deposit in the same block: the ORA-4 guard sees spot far from
the TWAP (the record moved at most 20 ticks), so the deposit reverts. The vault is never minted shares at the mark.

### test_slippage_theDefault300bpsCannotCoverThePoolFeeAndImpact

The pool charges 2.91 percent. The AgentTokenVault default slippage of 300 bps leaves 9 bps for price impact,
so a deposit that moves the price 10 bps reverts. The deploy script raises the bound to the 500 bps ceiling.
