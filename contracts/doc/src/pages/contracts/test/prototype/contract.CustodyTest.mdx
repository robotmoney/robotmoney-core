# CustodyTest

[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/d51a8ed56dc72ae8662acc53f88fb090d86e44cb/contracts/test/prototype/Custody.t.sol)

**Inherits:** [PrototypeHarness](/contracts/test/prototype/abstract.PrototypeHarness)

One-vault experiment (plan Step 2, G3; step 3d flows): the two custody invariants the other suites leave
open. INV-3: fee recipient, fee parameters, quarantine and the flow parameters change only through the
timelock. INV-2: donations accrue pro rata to holders (the seed's own shares included), and share
inflation by a first depositor does not pay (the seed, E14, makes the fund never empty).

<a id="test_inv2_firstdepositorinflationdoesnotpay"></a>

### test_INV2_firstDepositorInflationDoesNotPay

<i>

Classic first-depositor attack: mint a small position, donate to inflate the price, let the victim's
deposit round down. With the seed (E14) the fund is never empty, the 18-decimal offset still applies,
and the victim buys at the price of the settlement (its bound, E12), so the victim's loss is negligible
and the attack is a loss.

</i>

