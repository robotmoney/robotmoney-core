# OneVaultMockAavePool

[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/30d56e3e3fa551fc97da4f6d7e277f769b2d1a20/contracts/test/prototype/anvil/OneVaultKeeperAnvil.s.sol)

Test-only Aave pool: supply and withdraw move USDC one for one into an aToken. `setPaused` makes it refuse
supply and withdraw, and reports the reserve as paused, so the venue is closed the way a real pool would show it.
