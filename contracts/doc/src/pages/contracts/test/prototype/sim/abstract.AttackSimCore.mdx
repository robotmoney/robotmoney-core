**Title:** AttackSimCore

The attacker in the market loop, for both arms: its push toward a target price, its unwind, and the
result table each attack writes and scores. Harness-neutral (`SimMarket`).

## Structs

### Var

<i>

One variant of A2 or A10 (`ramp`): the flow aimed at, the pool, the level (bps, or bps per slot for a
ramp), the hold in seconds (A2) or the ramp's slots (A10), and whether the attacker trades (false: the
honest twin).

</i>

```solidity

        uint8 flow;
        uint8 b;
        uint16 lvl;
        uint32 hold;
        bool ramp;
        bool attack;
```
