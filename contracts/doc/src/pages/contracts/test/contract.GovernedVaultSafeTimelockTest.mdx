[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/32c74b4101392888212aa04d9da684c28a4aa416/contracts/test/GovernedVaultSafeTimelock.t.sol)

**Inherits:** [SafeGovernance](/contracts/test/helpers/abstract.SafeGovernance)

**Title:** GovernedVaultSafeTimelockTest

The vault admin setters (retire, setFeeRecipient, addAsset, removeAsset,
setExitFeeBps) on every governed vault family, driven the way production drives them:
two Safe owners sign, the real SafeL2 proxy runs `execTransaction`, the timelock waits
its delay, and the timelock calls the vault. Nothing pranks the Safe.
