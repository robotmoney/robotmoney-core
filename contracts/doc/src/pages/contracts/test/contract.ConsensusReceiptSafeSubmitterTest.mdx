# ConsensusReceiptSafeSubmitterTest

[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/5023b571e018713d76fbfec5a50bf139e4147d15/contracts/test/ConsensusReceiptSafeSubmitter.t.sol)

**Inherits:** [SafeGovernance](/contracts/test/helpers/abstract.SafeGovernance)

**Title:** ConsensusReceiptSafeSubmitterTest

The consensus receipt SUBMITTER is a real SafeL2 1.4.1 proxy (a second Safe, separate from the governing Safe). It holds
`RECEIPT_SUBMITTER_ROLE` on the receipt contract and nothing else. The role is granted by exactly one delayed timelock call
(`govern --row register-submitter`). The contract makes no tx.origin, isContract or ecrecover check on the role holder, so a
contract account is accepted unchanged, and the stored submitter is the Safe (`msg.sender`).
