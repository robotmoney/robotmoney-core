# IVenueHealthAdapter

[Git Source](https://github.com/robotmoney/robotmoney-core/blob/6e2b153decbfd1e3c9eb217de25017f3343488e4/contracts/interfaces/IVenueHealthAdapter.sol)

Strategy adapter that can report whether its venue still pays withdrawals
(`AaveV3Adapter`, `CompoundV3Adapter`).

<i>

`RobotMoneyVault` closes deposits (`maxDeposit` 0) while an active adapter reports false,
through `RobotMoneyVaultOps.anyActiveBlocked`, a `staticcall` that reads a missing or reverting
view as true. Minting while a venue cannot pay would count its stuck balance at face, and
the new USDC would fund early exits at that NAV (security round 3).

</i>

## Functions

<a id="venuewithdrawable"></a>

### venueWithdrawable

False while the venue refuses withdrawals: an Aave reserve that is paused or
inactive, a Comet that refuses withdrawals at the venue.
