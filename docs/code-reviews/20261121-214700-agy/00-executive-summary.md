> While `release-0.9.0` exhibits strong custody discipline on normal deposit, match, net-settlement, rebalance, and wind-down paths (`INV-1` non-arbitrary routing, `nonReentrant` coverage, `FlowMatchLib` cycle-factor accounting, and Check 10 checked 256-bit cross-multiplication failing closed on overflow above `2^256` all hold), the codebase contains **3 High** and **5 Medium** defects across emergency single-asset unwinds, timelock/keeper/role-floor governance, convergence math on delisted plans, residual token custody, and net-flow band extraction that must be resolved or formally accepted before scaling beyond the bounded `1,000 USDC` Deploy 4 rehearsal.

## Executive Answers to the Five Core Risk Questions

1. **Can governance be obviated or permanently bricked?**
   **Yes — across two contract-level governance floors (`AGY-02 [HIGH]`, `AGY-08 [MEDIUM]`) plus a 1-of-N keeper single-key takeover vector (`AGY-04 [MEDIUM]`).**
   - `ProposerFloorTimelock` (`contracts/ProposerFloorTimelock.sol:38-85`) enforces a last-holder floor only on `PROPOSER_ROLE` (`LastProposerFloor`), leaving `EXECUTOR_ROLE` unfloored. Under the Fund System's `D4-10` closed-executor policy (`FundTimelockPolicy.sol:17-19`, `DeployFundTimelock.s.sol:145-153`), the Safe is the sole `EXECUTOR_ROLE` holder. A single zero-delay `timelock.renounceRole(EXECUTOR_ROLE, safe)` transaction (or scheduled `revokeRole`) reduces `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to zero holders, permanently bricking `execute`/`executeBatch` across all 6 ERC-1967 proxies — including `RobotMoneyFund.shutdown()` (blocking `redeemInKind()` forever).
   - Self-administered `TARGET_SETTER_ROLE` (`RebalanceGovernance.sol:122`) and one-shot bound `GOVERNANCE_ROLE`, `REBALANCER_ROLE`, and `FLOW_ROLE` (`RobotMoneyFund.sol:112-114,284-292,518-530`) bypass `AdminFloorAccessControl` / `AdminFloorAccessControlCounter`, which check only `keccak256("ADMIN_ROLE")`. Dropping any of those roles — or freezing a bound proxy via `GovernedUUPS.freezeUpgrades` when replacement is later needed — leaves no `ADMIN_ROLE` recovery path (`AGY-08`).
   - `KeeperAccount` (`contracts/KeeperAccount.sol:52-55,77-98`) grants every owner unilateral 1-of-N authority. A single compromised owner EOA can add itself, eject all co-owners down to 1, and remove all bot keys, halting `FlowQueue.settle()` and `Rebalancer.step()` (which have no `keeperGrace` fallback under `D20`/`E34`) for a full `48h` timelock rotation window (`AGY-04`).
   - *Verified sound:* `PROPOSER_ROLE` (`ProposerFloorTimelock`), `ADMIN_ROLE` (`AdminFloorAccessControl` / `AdminFloorAccessControlCounter`), `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` (`ConsensusRecommendationReceipt`), and `GovernedUpgrade` (`upgradeTimelock` immutability + one-way `freezeUpgrades`) cannot be bypassed or bricked via last-admin renunciation.

2. **Is outright money theft possible?**
   **Arbitrary asset diversion (`INV-1`) is blocked across all paths, but 100% value extraction via an emergency single-asset unwind is possible (`AGY-01 [HIGH]`), and deactivated basket tokens can be permanently stranded (`AGY-07 [MEDIUM]`).**
   - `RobotMoneyFund.emergencyUnwind(uint256 index)` (`RobotMoneyFund.sol:879-881` → `FundFlowsLib.sol:678-701`) sets `floor = 0` whenever `FundValuationLib.feedPricePerToken` returns `feedOk == false`, executing a full-position market swap with `minOut = 0` (100% slippage/MEV sandwich loss allowed). Unlike full-fund `BasketSwaps.unwindAll` (`BasketSwaps.sol:189-195`, which requires a TWAP or configured floor and reverts `EmergencyFloorUnavailable`), single-asset unwind executes unprotected when a feed fails — the exact trigger condition for an emergency unwind.
   - All normal transfer sinks remain strict: swaps pay `address(this)`, `sweepForeignToken` transfers only to `ForeignTokenQuarantine.QUARANTINE`, and exclusion recoveries go strictly to `FundExclusionLedger`. However, because `RobotMoneyFund` omits `reabsorbRemovedAsset`, blocks `sweepForeignToken` on deactivated assets (`ProtectedToken`), and never reuses deactivated asset slots up to `MAX_FUND_ASSETS = 12`, post-deactivation basket balances can be stranded in violation of `INV-2` (`AGY-07`).

3. **Are there any exploitable reentrancy vulnerabilities?**
   **No exploitable reentrancy vector was identified (`AGY-14 [INFO]`).**
   - All user/keeper flow, swap, and receipt-application mutators across `RobotMoneyFund`, `FlowQueue`, `Rebalancer`, `RebalanceGovernance`, and `ConsensusRecommendationReceipt` are protected by `ReentrancyGuardUpgradeable` (`nonReentrant`), while role-gated/one-shot admin mutators and inherited ERC-20/AccessControl/UUPS entry points rely on CEI, role, and proxy guards. Internal queue side calls (`settleRedeemSide`, `settleDepositSide`) require `msg.sender == address(this)`, `FundExclusionLedger` writes require `onlyVault`, share burns precede external swap/transfer calls (`redeemInKind`, `_sellForFlow`), and `mintInKind`/`sellForFlow`/`payInKind` enforce balance-delta accounting plus Check 10's checked 256-bit cross-multiplication invariant (`FundValuationLib.requireProportion`, failing closed on overflow above `2^256`).

4. **Is economic manipulation possible?**
   **Yes — bounded net-flow extraction inside the `refBandBps = 90` bps feed tolerance (`AGY-06 [MEDIUM]`) and mathematical plan-convergence deadlock on delisted/unwound plans (`AGY-05 [MEDIUM]`).**
   - Slow pool price ramps (`A10`) and held TWAP pushes (`A2`) kept inside `refBandBps = 90` bps pass settlement Check 4 by construction and extract value from net-flow settlements (recorded as a documented Gate G8 failure on `release-0.9.0` in `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md:2345,2369`). Whole-match cross-user extraction (`A21`) is bounded by `matchBandBps = 10` bps.
   - `delistAsset(index)` and `emergencyUnwind(index)` zero `p.endBps[index]` on a live plan (`FundDelistLib.sol:113-121`, `FundFlowsLib.sol:650-658`) without adjusting `PlanMath`'s hardcoded `0` idle target (`PlanMath.sol:234,325-340`), making `PlanMath.withinTolerance` mathematically unreachable whenever the delisted weight exceeds `(n + 1) * toleranceBps` (`AGY-05`).

5. **Anything else critical (liveness, adapter/upgrade safety, product alignment, audit status)?**
   **Yes — a single-asset unwind liveness deadlock (`AGY-03 [HIGH]`), incomplete `ADR-0019` adapter vetting (`AGY-09 [LOW]`), missing Uniswap V3 factory verification (`AGY-13 [LOW]`), freeze/upgrade/empty-round edge cases (`AGY-10`, `AGY-11`, `AGY-12 [LOW]`), and unresolved PRD/audit-ledger drift (`AGY-15 [INFO]`).**
   - Calling `emergencyUnwind(index)` on a non-`inSale` basket asset sells the tokens but leaves `s.assets[index].active = true` (`FundFlowsLib.sol:681-701`). If that asset's feed is unhealthy, `totalAssets()`, `FlowQueue.settle()`, and `Rebalancer.step()` immediately brick (`FeedUnhealthy` / Check 2) while `PlanLive` blocks `deactivateAsset(index)` (`FundAdminLib.sol:550-556`) until governance closes the plan (`AGY-03`).
   - `FundAdapterGuard.vet` skips `codeHashAllowed` when `!s.adapterProxyCodeHash[h]`, and neither `StepFeedLib._twap` nor `FlowCheckLib._requireFundedPoolsFixedFee` calls `vet` (`AGY-09`).
   - Canonical `docs/prd.md` still specifies synchronous 4-vault withdrawals while the one-vault specification remains staged in `docs/technical/one-vault-prd-amendments.md`, and all 33 `v0.9.0` One-Vault contracts/libraries remain `Not separately audited` in `docs/audits.md` (`AGY-15`).

## Root-Cause Synthesis

Across the 15 findings, five structural patterns drive nearly all residual risk in `release-0.9.0`:

1. **Point-Solution Role Floors Instead of Systemic Role-Graph Invariants (`AGY-02`, `AGY-04`, `AGY-08`):**
   Last-holder floors were added incrementally for specific historical bugs (`PROPOSER_ROLE` in `ProposerFloorTimelock`, `ADMIN_ROLE` in `AdminFloorAccessControl*`, `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` in `ConsensusRecommendationReceipt`, and `LastOwner` in `KeeperAccount`), but when `D4-10` restricted `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to the Safe, when `RebalanceGovernance` and `RobotMoneyFund` introduced self-administered operational roles, and when `D36`/`D20` concentrated liveness in `KeeperAccount`, equivalent quorum/floor/recovery invariants were not applied uniformly.
2. **Asymmetric Degraded-Feed & Delisting State Transitions (`AGY-01`, `AGY-03`, `AGY-05`, `AGY-11`):**
   The `v0.9.0` delisting subsystem (`FundDelistLib`) correctly isolates `inSale` assets from healthy-feed checks (`Check 2`, `requireFeedsHealthy`) and deactivates them on completion (`closeDelistSale`), whereas the single-asset `emergencyUnwind(uint256)` path bypasses `inSale` state management (`active` stays `true` with `floor = 0` on dead feeds) and neither path reconciles `PlanMath`'s `0` idle end target when zeroing `p.endBps[index]`.
3. **Incomplete Single-Vault Port of Legacy `BasketVault` Custody Hygiene (`AGY-07`, `AGY-12`):**
   `BasketVault` enforced `LIFE-6` (`reabsorbRemovedAsset`) and slot reuse (`BasketAssetConfigGuard.reuseOrRejectDuplicate`). When `RobotMoneyFund` unified lending and basket assets under a 12-slot array (`MAX_FUND_ASSETS = 12`), slot reuse and removed-asset reabsorption were dropped while `ProtectedToken` remained unconditional.
4. **Cross-Contract Boundary Omissions under `ADR-0019` and Hardening Bounds (`AGY-06`, `AGY-09`, `AGY-10`, `AGY-13`):**
   Because `RobotMoneyFund`, `FlowQueue`, and `Rebalancer` operate near the 24,576-byte EIP-170 ceiling (`FlowQueue` sits ~257 bytes below EIP-170 per `one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md:2343`), adapter vetting (`FundAdapterGuard.vet`) and canonical pool validation were omitted across library/queue boundaries (`StepFeedLib`, `FlowCheckLib`, `UniswapV3ExactOutSwapAdapter`), while `FlowQueue` state bounds (`maxDepositMinHold`, `refBandBps`) leave residual griefing or extraction windows.
5. **Canon & Audit-Ledger Lag Behind Rapid Prototype-to-Release Promotion (`AGY-15`, balanced by `AGY-14`):**
   Moving the 33 one-vault files out of `contracts/test/prototype/src/` into `contracts/` preserved strong local accounting invariants (`AGY-14`), but canonical PRD synchronization and external audit sign-off remain pending.
