## 1. Operational Failure Modes & Recovery Analysis

### 1.1 Keeper Liveness, Gas Funding, and the `D20` vs. `DEP-03` Contradiction
- **Owner policy boundary (Rule 1, `D20`, `D24`):** Under Owner Rule 1 (§19.1 L2882–2885) and `D20` (§5.11.3 item 5 L1039–1045; §5.11.6 L1246; §11 L1895), the keeper is explicitly a team-operated non-actor, `keeperGrace` is intentionally removed, and `settle()`, `step()`, and delisted-asset sales (§5.11.6 L1259) are strictly `KEEPER_ROLE`-only. Under `D24` (§5.11.5 L1182–1190; §11 L1899; §19.2 L2897), no permissionless trigger may force a wind-down.
- **No user escape hatch during a keeper outage:** Because `cancel` closes at epoch close (`RB-25`, §5.11.3 item 3 L1031–1035), `redeemInKind` is disabled while the fund runs (`D18`, §5.11.5 L1177; §11 L1893), and a keeper outage does not trigger a deposit stop (§5.11.4 L1081–1090), locked depositors and redeemers cannot exit while the keeper is down. Deposits expire only after `maxRoll` epochs (48h at 8 × 6h defaults, §5.9.5 L809, L814), and redemptions never expire (`D25` L1900; §5.11.3 item 7 L1052–1056)—after `maxRoll` epochs holders can only give notice to receive shares back at the end of the next round, remaining trapped inside a non-settling fund.
- **`D20` multisig recommendation vs. `DEP-03` single-holder mandate:** `D20` (§5.11.3 item 5 L1039–1040; §19.1 L2882–2883) states *"The keeper is a team-operated account, preferably a multisig,"* while `DEP-03` (§5.11.9 L1355; `one-vault-timed-flows-eng-plan.md` §8.1 E47 L414) mandates *"The keeper is the only `KEEPER_ROLE` holder."* This leaves two conflicting operational modes:
  1. A human multisig cannot practically co-sign automated 30-minute `step()` calls (`stepInterval`, §5.6 L525) and multi-block `settle()` calls (`basketCallBps = 5`, §5.9.5 L824; §14 L2183–2184).
  2. If the single `KEEPER_ROLE` holder is instead a single EOA bot key (§5.6 L549; §8 L1591–1592), any bot host crash, RPC failure, key loss, or gas depletion—noting `v0.8.0-onevault` verifies keeper gas only before its first send at deploy time (§10 L1674–1676)—halts all settlements and rebalances until `ADMIN_ROLE` completes a **48-hour Safe-only timelock rotation** (`D4-10`, §5.6 L562–565; §5.8 L593; `ADR-0004` L190–196, L266–268).

### 1.2 Safe Multisig, Timelock (`D4-10`/`D24`/`D30`), and `EMERGENCY_ROLE` Cooldowns (`D29`/`RB-32`)
- **Intentional timelocked authority split:** By owner decision (`D4-10`, `D24`, `D30`), `EMERGENCY_ROLE` holds only self-expiring pauses (`pauseDeposits`, `pauseRebalance`, `pauseQueue`, `freezeSettlement`), while `ADMIN_ROLE` (Safe + 48h timelock + Safe-only executor) holds `shutdown`, `restore`, `emergencyUnwind` (including single-asset unwind, §5.11.5 L1210–1213), delisting (`D30`), unpauses, `releaseSettlement`, and role grants (§5.6 L545–565; §5.11.5 L1179–1236; §11 L1899, L1905; §18.6 L2855–2857). Waiting 48h for `shutdown`, `emergencyUnwind`, or delisting is accepted owner policy rather than an accidental omission.
- **Runbook-only wind-down notice protection vs. `maxPause` cooldown deadlock (`OPS-02`):** Scheduling `shutdown` makes the wind-down public for the 48h timelock delay. To prevent redeemers from matching at NAV against locked depositors during notice, §5.11.5 (L1190–1197) relies on a **runbook rule** (`D29` L1904; §21.2 L3045) to call `pauseDeposits` alongside scheduling `shutdown`. However, under `D29` / `RB-32` (§5.11.5 L1218–1219; §7 L1543–1545), `pauseDeposits` auto-expires after `maxPause` (72h default) and **cannot be set again for another `maxPause` (72h)**. If an earlier `pauseDeposits` just expired within 72h, `EMERGENCY_ROLE` is blocked on-chain from pausing deposits when `shutdown` is proposed. Furthermore, at the minimum boundary `maxPause == timelockDelay` permitted by `RB-32` (§5.11.5 L1220–1221; §7 L1545; `E51` L418), any timing gap between `pauseDeposits` and Safe execution of `shutdown` after the 48h timelock lets the pause auto-expire *before* `shutdown` executes.
- **Remaining operational defects under `D4-10` / `D24` (`OPS-03`):** (a) Revoking a compromised `EMERGENCY_ROLE` through the 48h timelock leaves active 72h pauses and 7-day freezes (`maxFreeze`, §5.9.5 L816) in place unless atomically batched with `unpause*` and `releaseSettlement` (§5.11.5 L1233–1236); (b) §5.6 (L547–548) lists `pauseRebalance` on `EMERGENCY_ROLE` and `resumePlan` on `ADMIN_ROLE` without listing `unpauseRebalance` (and keeps `unpauseQueue` despite `pauseQueue` now self-expiring per §5.11.8 L1311); and (c) Safe two-step propose + execute coordination under `D4-10` lacks signer-availability SLAs—if Safe signers take >24h to execute after the 48h delay, a 72h `maxPause` expires before timelocked mitigations take effect.

### 1.3 Off-Chain Coupling, `v0.8.0`/`v0.9.0` Reconciliation, and Migration Obligations
- **Receipt-to-target semantic gap (`OPS-06`):** `RebalanceGovernance.applyReceipt` does not derive or verify the `(assets, bps)` vector from the receipt payload on-chain (§20.4 L3002–3005; `09-receipt-and-economic-challenge.md` L79–85), and `D8` (§11 L1883; §8 L1564–1565) leaves per-asset weight authority open for production.
- **Client & monitoring surface expansion (`OPS-07`):** §8 (L1554–1593) adds request/claim/cancel/notice flows across dApp, `rmpc`, gateway, indexer, Watchdog, and `publish-contracts`. Users have no price quote (`quoteDeposit`/`quoteRedeem` return terms only, §5.11.3 item 2 L1027–1030) and depend on off-chain UI/Watchdog alerts to catch automatic deposit-stop resumptions before an epoch closes (§5.11.3 item 8 L1057–1063).
- **`v0.8.0` vs. `v0.9.0` spec reconciliation (`OPS-05`):** `§5.10` (L934–935) and `§5.11` (L941–946) explicitly retain `§5.6` and `§5.9` as the `v0.8.0-onevault` baseline until Step 3f (`v0.9.0-onevault`) is built, and `§5.11.8` (L1311) explicitly reconciles `§5.6`'s three changed roles rows (`keeperGrace`, `allowReturn`, `pauseQueue`). Only `§8` (L1590, listing `allow-settlement-return` without a Step 3f removal tag) and the absence of a forward pointer in `§5.6` (L533, L547, L549) / `§5.9.5` (L810) to `§5.11.8` remain un-annotated.
- **Retired vaults (`OPS-08`):** §9 item 6 (L1613–1614) requires maintaining TWAP pools and V3 price sources for retired vaults (`rmUSDC`, `rmPROTO`, `rmRWA`) indefinitely post-migration.

## 3. Numbered Findings

### OPS-01 — High: Operational Contradiction Between `D20` (Team Multisig Keeper) and `DEP-03` (Single `KEEPER_ROLE` Holder) Under `D4-10`
- **Citations:** `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md` §5.6 (L525, L549, L562–565), §5.8 (L593), §5.9.5 (L824), §5.11.3 item 5 (L1039–1045), §5.11.5 (L1177, L1182–1190), §5.11.9 (L1355), §8 (L1582, L1591–1592), §10 (L1674–1676), §11 `D20` (L1895), `D24` (L1899), §14 (L2183–2184), §19.1 Rule 1 (L2882–2885), §19.2 (L2897); `docs/technical/one-vault-timed-flows-eng-plan.md` §8.1 `E47` (L414); `docs/adr/ADR-0004-agent-token-shortlist-governance.md` (L190–196, L266–268); `docs/code-reviews/0.7.0/08-deployment-challenge.md` (L52–86).
- **Problem Description:** Within Owner Rule 1 (§19.1 L2882–2885), `D20` (§11 L1895), and `D24` (§11 L1899), the keeper is a team-operated non-actor, `keeperGrace` is intentionally removed, and no permissionless fallback or wind-down exists. However, `D20` (§5.11.3 item 5 L1039–1040) directly contradicts `DEP-03` (§5.11.9 L1355 / `E47` L414):
  1. `D20` specifies *"The keeper is a team-operated account, preferably a multisig,"* yet human multisig signers cannot co-sign automated 30-minute `step()` calls (`stepInterval`, §5.6 L525) and multi-block `settle()` calls (`basketCallBps = 5`, §5.9.5 L824; §14 L2183–2184).
  2. Conversely, if the single `KEEPER_ROLE` address mandated by `DEP-03` (`"The keeper is the only KEEPER_ROLE holder"`) is an automated single-EOA bot key (§5.6 L549; §8 L1591–1592), any bot host failure, RPC outage, key loss, or gas exhaustion—while `v0.8.0-onevault` only validates keeper gas at deploy time (§10 L1674–1676)—leaves zero authorized fallback callers and forces a **48-hour Safe-only timelock rotation** (`D4-10`, §5.6 L562–565) via `ADMIN_ROLE`.
- **Impact:** Without a non-timelocked keeper failover path that respects `D20` and `D24`, any single bot outage or key loss stalls all settlements, rebalances, and delisting sales for at least **48 hours** (timelock delay + two rounds of Safe signatures), during which users have no post-cutoff `cancel` (`RB-25`) or running-fund `redeemInKind` (`D18`).
- **Recommendation (preserving Rule 1, `D20`, and `D24`):**
  1. **Satisfy both `D20` and `DEP-03` via a Keeper Smart Account:** Make the single `KEEPER_ROLE` holder a dedicated **Keeper Safe / smart account equipped with a 1-of-N automated bot session-key module** controlled by the team Safe, enabling instant hot-standby bot failover and instant compromised-key revocation without a 48h timelock delay (and ensure `DEP-03`'s Safe module check distinguishes the governance Safe's zero-module policy from the Keeper Safe's attested session-key module); **OR** amend `DEP-03` (§5.11.9 L1355 and `E47` L414) to explicitly permit a **2-key pre-attested primary + standby team keeper EOA pair** (`keeperCount() <= 2`, both verified by `verify/fund.ts`).
  2. Specify automated keeper gas-balance monitoring, alert thresholds, and top-up automation in the Watchdog/Keeper runbook (§8 L1582, L1591–1592).

### OPS-02 — High: `maxPause` Cooldown Deadlocks the Wind-Down Notice Protection (`shutdown` + `pauseDeposits`)
- **Citations:** `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md` §5.11.5 (L1190–1197, L1217–1222), §7 `RB-32` (L1543–1545), §11 `D24` (L1899), `D29` (L1904), §21.2 (L3045); `docs/technical/one-vault-timed-flows-eng-plan.md` §8.1 `E39` (L406), `E51` (L418).
- **Problem Description:** Scheduling `shutdown` exposes the upcoming wind-down for the 48-hour timelock delay. To prevent redeemers from matching at NAV against locked depositors during this notice window, §5.11.5 (L1192–1197) relies on a **runbook rule** (`D29` L1904; §21.2 L3045) to call `pauseDeposits` alongside scheduling `shutdown`. However, under `D29` / `RB-32` (§5.11.5 L1218–1219; §7 L1543–1545), `pauseDeposits` auto-expires after `maxPause` (72h default) and **cannot be set again for another `maxPause` (72h)**. Furthermore, `RB-32` / `E51` only enforces `maxPause >= timelockDelay`, permitting the **`maxPause == timelockDelay` edge case** (e.g., 48h == 48h): unless `pauseDeposits` is called simultaneously with or after `shutdown` scheduling and Safe signers execute `shutdown` at the exact instant the timelock matures under `D4-10`, the pause auto-expires *before* `shutdown` executes.
- **Impact:** If `EMERGENCY_ROLE` used `pauseDeposits` earlier in an escalating incident and that pause expired within the last `maxPause` (72h), `EMERGENCY_ROLE` is locked out of calling `pauseDeposits` when the Safe schedules `shutdown`. Operators must then either delay wind-down by up to 72 hours or expose locked depositors to 48 hours of adverse-selection NAV matching; similarly, at `maxPause == timelockDelay` (or with any >24h Safe execution lag at 72h default), notice protection lapses mid-timelock.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Enforce wind-down deposit protection on-chain rather than by runbook—for example, by allowing `ADMIN_ROLE` to set a non-expiring `windDownPending` / `adminPauseDeposits` state when scheduling `shutdown` that is exempt from `EMERGENCY_ROLE`'s `RB-32` cooldown.
  2. Strengthen the `RB-32` / `E51` lower bound from `maxPause >= timelockDelay` to `maxPause >= timelockDelay + epochLength` (e.g., ≥54h at 48h timelock + 6h epoch) if `EMERGENCY_ROLE` pauses remain part of wind-down notice coverage.

### OPS-03 — Medium: Operational Defects Under Intentional 48h Safe-Only Timelock Governance (`D4-10`, `D24`, `D30`)
- **Citations:** `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md` §5.6 (L541–565), §5.9.5 (L816), §5.11.5 (L1182–1236), §5.11.8 (L1308–1311), §10 (L1672–1681), §11 `D24` (L1899), `D30` (L1905), §18.6 (L2855–2857), §19.2 (L2897); `docs/adr/ADR-0004-agent-token-shortlist-governance.md` (L266–268).
- **Problem Description:** The 48-hour Safe-only timelock delay on `shutdown` and `emergencyUnwind` is an **intentional owner decision** (`D4-10`, `D24`, `D30`; §18.6 L2855–2857; §19.2 L2897). Within that accepted governance model, three concrete operational defects remain:
  1. **Compromised `EMERGENCY_ROLE` cleanup persistence:** A compromised `EMERGENCY_ROLE` can halt deposits, queue, and rebalancer for `maxPause` (72h) and freeze one settlement's deposit shares for `maxFreeze` (7 days default, §5.9.5 L816). Revoking `EMERGENCY_ROLE` through the 48h timelock leaves those active pauses and freezes in place unless explicitly cleared by `ADMIN_ROLE` (`unpause*` / `releaseSettlement`, §5.11.5 L1233–1236).
  2. **`unpauseRebalance` vs. `resumePlan` ambiguity (`§5.6 L547`):** §5.6 (L548) grants `pauseRebalance` to `EMERGENCY_ROLE`, while §5.6 (L547) lists `resumePlan` under `ADMIN_ROLE` without listing `unpauseRebalance` (and lists `unpauseQueue`, even though §5.11.8 L1311 states `pauseQueue` ends by itself).
  3. **Two-step Safe execution SLA gap (`D4-10`):** Because `D4-10` (§5.6 L562–565; §5.11.8 L1309; `ADR-0004` L266–268) makes the Safe the timelock's *only* executor, every non-pause action (`shutdown`, `emergencyUnwind`, delisting, role revocation, unpauses) requires Safe quorum to propose, a 48h wait, and Safe quorum *again* to execute—with no documented signer-availability SLAs to ensure execution completes before a 72h `maxPause` expires.
- **Impact:** Delayed or fragmented cleanup after an `EMERGENCY_ROLE` compromise (leaving a 7-day freeze or 72h pause running past revocation), governance tooling ambiguity over `unpauseRebalance` vs. `resumePlan`, and risk of emergency pauses expiring if Safe execution coordination lags past 72h.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Support and document atomic `TimelockController` batching (`scheduleBatch`/`executeBatch`) to revoke a compromised `EMERGENCY_ROLE` and clear active pauses/`releaseSettlement` in a single 48h timelock cycle.
  2. Clarify `unpauseRebalance` vs. `resumePlan` (and `unpauseQueue`/`unpauseDeposits` early-override semantics) in §5.6 (L547).
  3. Document Safe signer-availability SLAs and pre-staged execution procedures for the two-step propose + execute requirement under `D4-10`.

### OPS-04 — High: Step 3f `RUN-01` 48h Redemption Strand and Deferred `v0.9.0-onevault` Verification Gaps (`DEP-01`–`03`, `SIM-01`–`02`, `ARCH-08`)
- **Citations:** `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md` §4 (L108), §4.5 rule 5 & rule 6 (L325–360), §5.6 (L550–551), §5.9.3 checks 8 & 11 (L738–741, L768–770), §5.11.3 item 3 (L1031–1035), §5.11.4 (L1081–1082, L1088–1089, L1128–1136, L1160–1161), §5.11.5 (L1177), §5.11.9 (L1322–1376), §7 `RB-25`, `RB-26`, `RB-28`, `RB-33` (L1519–1525, L1531–1534, L1546–1548), §10 (L1788–1789, L1847–1852), §11 `D18`, `D30` (L1893, L1905), §20 (L2951–3012); `docs/technical/one-vault-timed-flows-eng-plan.md` §8.1 `E33`, `E43`–`E48` (L410–415, L419); `docs/code-reviews/0.7.0/01-findings-index.md` (L1–30), `08-deployment-challenge.md` (L1–109), `09-receipt-and-economic-challenge.md` (L1–114), `10-recovery-and-accounting.md` (L1–19); `docs/code-reviews/design-review-0.8.0/01-architecture-and-coherence.md` (`ARCH-08`, L146–156).
- **Problem Description:**
  1. **Step 3f `RUN-01` 48-hour redemption strand (elevates `OPS-04` to High):** Under §4.5 rule 6 (L350–352) and `RB-33` (L1546–1548), each round re-reads active pools' fees; when a pool's fee rises so `poolFee + feeMarginBps >= legBandBps` (or `poolFee >= legBandBps`), §5.11.4 (L1088–1089) **stops deposits and the match**, which forces all queued redemptions into **100% net redemptions** (§5.11.4 L1081–1082). Because an asset is never delisted automatically (`D30`, §4.5 rule 5 L325; §11 L1905), those net redemptions must sell a pro-rata slice of that exact over-fee pool (§5.11.4 L1128–1132). Under §5.9.3 Check 8 (L738–741) and Step 3f Check 11 (§5.9.3 L768–770; §5.11.4 L1160–1161; `E33` L419), every sale leg must achieve at least `max(feed, TWAP) * (1 - legBandBps)`. When `poolFee >= legBandBps` (or `poolFee + priceImpact > legBandBps` where `poolFee + feeMarginBps >= legBandBps`), the sale immediately reverts on Check 8 / Check 11—and under Step 3f (`D18`, `RB-26`, `RB-25`), redeemers have neither running-fund `redeemInKind` (§5.11.5 L1177) nor post-cutoff `cancel` (§5.11.3 item 3 L1031–1035).
  2. **Incomplete `DEP-03` role-exclusivity scope (`ARCH-08` cross-reference):** While §20.5 (L3007–3011) confirms `v0.8.0-onevault` was not run-verified for `DEP-01` or `DEP-03` beyond the executor check, `§5.11.9` `DEP-03` (L1353–1357) / `E47` (L414) checks exclusivity only for timelock proposer/canceller/executor, `ADMIN_ROLE`, `KEEPER_ROLE`, and `EMERGENCY_ROLE`—omitting internal and governance roles flagged in `ARCH-08` (L146–156): `FLOW_ROLE`, `REBALANCER_ROLE`, `GOVERNANCE_ROLE` (§5.6 L550), `TARGET_SETTER_ROLE` (§5.6 L551), and `RECEIPT_SUBMITTER_ROLE` (§4 L108), as well as UUPS upgrade authority.

### OPS-06 — Medium: Off-Chain Semantic Binding & Unresolved Per-Asset Weight Authority (D8)

### OPS-07 — Low: Automatic Deposit-Stop Resumption Creates Cancel-Window Coupling for Depositors
- **Citations:** `docs/technical/one-vault-rebalancer-proposal.md` §5.11.3 item 8 (L1057–1063), §5.11.4 (L1081–1090), §8 (L1571–1583).
- **Problem Description:** Automatic deposit stops (e.g., D21 venue illiquidity or §4.5 rule 6 pool-fee spike) can end mid-epoch without a timelock, immediately folding remaining deposits into the open epoch with cancellation closing at that epoch's end (§5.11.3 item 8).
- **Impact:** If the dApp, indexer, or Watchdog (§8) lags, depositors intending to cancel during a stop may miss the epoch close and be locked into forward-priced settlement.
- **Recommendation:** Ensure Watchdog and dApp prominently surface deposit-stop transitions and exact epoch-close cancel deadlines.
