# Vaults and adapters — source review

Review base: `3c753ff5b72f828f082673d4c5983418a418c47e` (15 October 2026). This chapter is a source review of `RobotMoneyVault`, `BasketVault` and its three subclasses, the three lending adapters, and the linked vault operations. Findings below have not yet been reproduced on a fork or validated against a deployed proxy. A source finding is not a claim that a deployed vault presently has the triggering state.

## Findings

### VA-01 — Medium: basket `mint` does not mint the requested number of shares

`BasketVault.mint(shares, receiver)` passes `shares` to the inherited ERC-4626 entrypoint, which charges `previewMint(shares)` assets ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:561`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). `previewMint` grosses the charge up for worst-case slippage ([`:846`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). The internal `_deposit` explicitly discards the requested share count and mints shares from the **actual** post-swap NAV increase ([`:592`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:650`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:660`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). Unlike `deposit`, `mint` has no override of the inherited returned asset amount or a check that the exact requested shares were issued. Its `Deposit` event reports the actual count, while callers of `mint` normally assume the requested count.

Concrete sequence: with a 1% slippage bound, `previewMint(100 shares)` charges roughly 101 USDC of pre-swap NAV value. If the swap realizes the full 101 USDC at TWAP, `_deposit` mints roughly 101 shares, although the caller requested 100. A strategy that calls `mint(100)` to hit an exact share position receives a larger position; an allowance or accounting wrapper may record 100 while custody holds more. The extra shares are backed by the extra value paid, so this is an interface/integration fault rather than demonstrated dilution. The existing `test_previewMint_notCheaperThanDeposit_dilutionPrevented` checks preview arithmetic only ([`contracts/test/BasketVault.t.sol:2006`](../../../contracts/test/BasketVault.t.sol)); it does not call `mint` and assert the receiver's balance delta. Preserve realized-value accounting while giving `mint` an explicit, documented exact-share behavior, or disable it with a dedicated error as already done for `withdraw`.

### VA-02 — Low: rmUSDC `maxWithdraw` can quote an unfillable venue balance

`RobotMoneyVault.maxWithdraw` converts the holder's shares against reported NAV and deducts the exit fee, without limiting the result to idle USDC plus immediately withdrawable adapter assets ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:668`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). `totalAssets` includes each eligible active adapter's reported position ([`:543`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). An actual withdrawal must pull the shortfall from adapters and reverts if they fail or cannot deliver it ([`:796`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol), [`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:415`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`:463`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Aave and Morpho position value can exceed presently available underlying liquidity; adapter withdrawal uses the venue call and propagates its failure ([`contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol:143`](../../../contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol:147`](../../../contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol)).

Concrete sequence: the holder owns all shares, nearly all USDC is deployed to a lending market, and that market has insufficient liquid USDC for full redemption. `maxWithdraw(holder)` still returns the holder's full NAV share net of fee; `withdraw(maxWithdraw(holder))` reverts at the venue. A caller using the max view as an executable limit cannot know the payable amount. This does not establish an avoidable liquidity loss; it is a misleading ERC-4626 limit under market stress. A conservative liquidity view or explicit documented best-effort limit would address the integration risk. This differs from June finding M-2, which concerned rounding with a nonzero fee.

## Rechecked historical issues and controls

- June H-1 (`previewMint` undercharging against the deposit haircut): the current `previewMint` applies the slippage gross-up ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:846`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). VA-01 is a distinct exact-share consequence of the later realized-value mint logic.
- June M-8 (basket exact-asset `withdraw`): `previewWithdraw` now reverts `RedeemOnly`; `redeem` returns the measured post-swap payout ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:732`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:885`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). This is an intentional redeem-only surface, though generic ERC-4626 clients need to recognize it.
- June M-2 (exit-fee rounding): current `maxWithdraw` rounds net proceeds down, while `previewWithdraw` grosses them up ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:656`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol), [`:668`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). The old arithmetic flaw is addressed on source inspection; VA-02 concerns venue liquidity.
- October security review's excluded-adapter recovery concerns: current NAV and proportional pulls share `isCounted`, which rejects an open exclusion ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:168`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:543`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)); force removal opens or retains a snapshot record, and re-add drains it before pushing a new active entry ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:668`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`:501`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Recovery credits the ledger from the observed USDC balance delta ([`:1021`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). These controls are present in source; their deployment and adverse-state behavior remain unverified.
- Adapter eligibility requires an address allowlist, runtime code-hash allowlist, exact USDC and bound vault views ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:145`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Keeper rebalance checks deposit pause and shutdown before moving capital ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:992`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). Aave/Compound/Morpho supply refusals hand USDC back to the vault; Aave and Compound expose withdrawal-health reads ([`contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol:107`](../../../contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol:102`](../../../contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol:119`](../../../contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol)).

## Remaining verification

Reproduce VA-01 with a successful `mint` at a spot outcome above the slippage floor and assert requested shares versus receiver balance delta. Reproduce VA-02 on a fork or a lending mock that reports a positive position but refuses the full withdrawal. Exercise exclusion/recovery with partial venue liquidity, `totalAssets` reverting, repeated force-remove/re-add, prior holder exit, and zero supply. Confirm deployed proxy implementations, adapter code hashes, venue configuration, and actual caps before any release verdict. This review did not execute tests or inspect deployed bytecode.
