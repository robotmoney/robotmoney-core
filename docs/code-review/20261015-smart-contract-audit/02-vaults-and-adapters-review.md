# Vaults and adapters — source review

Review base: `3c753ff5b72f828f082673d4c5983418a418c47e` (15 October 2026). This chapter is a source review of `RobotMoneyVault`, `BasketVault` and its three subclasses, the three lending adapters, and the linked vault operations. Findings below have not yet been reproduced on a fork or validated against a deployed proxy. A source finding is not a claim that a deployed vault presently has the triggering state.

## Findings

### VA-01 — Low (interface; formerly Medium): basket `mint` does not mint the requested number of shares

**Disposition after design review:** Retained with narrower wording. Minting from the realized NAV delta is intended: the [PRD](../../prd.md) states that vault shares are minted and redeemed in proportion to realized NAV delta. The remaining issue is that `mint(shares)` issues a different share count from the one requested. That is an ERC-4626 interface mismatch for integrators. The report has not shown a security impact that justifies Medium.

`BasketVault.mint(shares, receiver)` passes `shares` to the inherited ERC-4626 entrypoint, which charges `previewMint(shares)` assets ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:561`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). `previewMint` grosses the charge up for worst-case slippage ([`:846`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). The internal `_deposit` explicitly discards the requested share count and mints shares from the **actual** post-swap NAV increase ([`:592`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:650`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:660`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). Unlike `deposit`, `mint` has no override of the inherited returned asset amount or a check that the exact requested shares were issued. Its `Deposit` event reports the actual count, while callers of `mint` normally assume the requested count.

Concrete sequence: with a 1% slippage bound, `previewMint(100 shares)` charges roughly 101 USDC of pre-swap NAV value. If the swap realizes the full 101 USDC at TWAP, `_deposit` mints roughly 101 shares, although the caller requested 100. A strategy that calls `mint(100)` to hit an exact share position receives a larger position; an allowance or accounting wrapper may record 100 while custody holds more. The extra shares are backed by the extra value paid, so this is an interface/integration fault rather than demonstrated dilution. The existing `test_previewMint_notCheaperThanDeposit_dilutionPrevented` checks preview arithmetic only ([`contracts/test/BasketVault.t.sol:2006`](../../../contracts/test/BasketVault.t.sol)); it does not call `mint` and assert the receiver's balance delta. Preserve realized-value accounting while giving `mint` an explicit, documented exact-share behavior, or disable it with a dedicated error as already done for `withdraw`.

### VA-02 — Low (integration): rmUSDC `maxWithdraw` and `maxRedeem` can overstate executable capacity

**Disposition after design review:** Partially reclassified. Venue illiquidity and the resulting withdrawal revert are a known design gap. [Asset valuation](../../technical/asset-valuation.md) lists "adapter liquidity vs. NAV disparity" and names `InsufficientAdapterLiquidity` as the protocol's acknowledgment of it. That part is not a new vulnerability. The narrower claim stays open: the two max views can report more than a caller can execute without revert. Accepting illiquidity does not resolve that ERC-4626 discrepancy.

`RobotMoneyVault.maxWithdraw` converts the holder's shares against reported NAV and deducts the exit fee, without limiting the result to idle USDC plus immediately withdrawable adapter assets ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:668`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). `totalAssets` includes each eligible active adapter's reported position ([`:543`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). An actual withdrawal must pull the shortfall from adapters and reverts if they fail or cannot deliver it ([`:796`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol), [`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:415`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`:463`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Aave and Morpho position value can exceed presently available underlying liquidity; adapter withdrawal uses the venue call and propagates its failure ([`contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol:143`](../../../contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol:147`](../../../contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol)).

Concrete sequence: the holder owns all shares, nearly all USDC is deployed to a lending market, and that market has insufficient liquid USDC for full redemption. `maxWithdraw(holder)` still returns the holder's full NAV share net of fee; `withdraw(maxWithdraw(holder))` reverts at the venue. The inherited OpenZeppelin `maxRedeem(owner)` returns the entire share balance ([`ERC4626Upgradeable.sol:169`](../../../lib/openzeppelin-contracts-upgradeable/contracts/token/ERC20/extensions/ERC4626Upgradeable.sol)), so it can similarly overstate executable redemption capacity. A caller using either max view as an executable limit cannot know the payable amount. ERC-4626 defines these views as maxima that must not exceed the amount accepted without revert ([specification](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626)). This does not establish an avoidable liquidity loss; it is a potentially misleading integration limit under market stress. The market's liquidity is dynamic and may make a perfectly accurate view impractical; test venue-specific behavior before recommending a code fix. This differs from June finding M-2, which concerned rounding with a nonzero fee.

### VA-03 — Low candidate: a positive redeem preview can be followed by a smaller payout when legs are skipped as dust

**Disposition after design review:** Narrowed. Skipping a dust leg is intended behavior. The remaining candidate is a positive `previewRedeem` followed by a smaller actual payout. That conflicts with the worst-case floor promise in [ADR-0003](../../adr/ADR-0003-slippage-adjusted-basket-vault-preview.md) §1. It still needs reproduction.

`previewRedeem` discounts the aggregate share value ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:796`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)), while `_sellProportional` computes a sale floor for each leg using `sellAmount - 1` and skips each leg whose floor rounds to zero ([`:934`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). If a final holder owns all shares, the vault has no idle USDC, and its remaining active assets consist of multiple tiny legs, the aggregate preview can round to a positive USDC amount while each leg is skipped and the actual payout is zero. The shares are burned in `_withdraw` ([`:912`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)).

The current V4 dust regression covers one dust leg with idle USDC and verifies that idle USDC is returned ([`contracts/test/BasketVaultV4Venue.t.sol:406`](../../../contracts/test/BasketVaultV4Venue.t.sol)); it does not test a positive aggregate preview with every leg skipped. `redeemInKind` may provide an exit for supported holders, so quantify affected configurations and whether the precise preview discrepancy violates the intended interface before assigning final severity. Reproduce with multiple individually sub-unit-valued legs, zero idle USDC, full-supply redemption, and nonzero aggregate NAV; assert `previewRedeem`, returned assets, receiver balance, and residual vault balances.

## Rechecked historical issues and controls

- June H-1 (`previewMint` undercharging against the deposit haircut): the current `previewMint` applies the slippage gross-up ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:846`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). VA-01 is a distinct exact-share consequence of the later realized-value mint logic.
- June M-8 (basket exact-asset `withdraw`): `previewWithdraw` now reverts `RedeemOnly`; `redeem` returns the measured post-swap payout ([`contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol:732`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol), [`:885`](../../../contracts/vaults/BasketVault.sol)). This is an intentional redeem-only surface, though generic ERC-4626 clients need to recognize it.
- June M-2 (exit-fee rounding): current `maxWithdraw` rounds net proceeds down, while `previewWithdraw` grosses them up ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:656`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol), [`:668`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). The old arithmetic flaw is addressed on source inspection; VA-02 concerns venue liquidity.
- October security review's excluded-adapter recovery concerns: current NAV and proportional pulls share `isCounted`, which rejects an open exclusion ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:168`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:543`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)); force removal opens or retains a snapshot record, and re-add drains it before pushing a new active entry ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:668`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol), [`:501`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Recovery credits the ledger from the observed USDC balance delta ([`:1021`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). These controls are present in source; their deployment and adverse-state behavior remain unverified.
- Adapter eligibility requires an address allowlist, runtime code-hash allowlist, exact USDC and bound vault views ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:145`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)). Keeper rebalance checks deposit pause and shutdown before moving capital ([`contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol:992`](../../../contracts/RobotMoneyVault.sol)). Aave/Compound/Morpho supply refusals hand USDC back to the vault; Aave and Compound expose withdrawal-health reads ([`contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol:107`](../../../contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol:102`](../../../contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol:119`](../../../contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol)).
- Exclusion closeout is an assumption, not a finding. `_restore` finalizes an open record after `_drain` reports no revert ([`contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol:1006-1015`](../../../contracts/lib/RobotMoneyVaultOps.sol)); no post-drain balance assertion appears in that path. No current failure is reachable: all three supported adapters revert `WithdrawShortfall` on a short withdrawal ([`contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol:146`](../../../contracts/adapters/AaveV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol:153`](../../../contracts/adapters/CompoundV3Adapter.sol), [`contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol:160`](../../../contracts/adapters/MorphoAdapter.sol)), and the code-hash allowlist admits no other adapter. Record the assumption that every future allowlisted adapter keeps that revert. `ExclusionLedger.claimAll` loops over all records ([`contracts/ExclusionLedger.sol:271-275`](../../../contracts/ExclusionLedger.sol)); individual `claim(id, holder)` is the escape if aggregate claiming becomes too costly, so record this as an operational limit.

## Remaining verification

Reproduce VA-01 with a successful `mint` at a spot outcome above the slippage floor and assert requested shares versus receiver balance delta. Reproduce VA-02 on a lending mock that reports a positive position but refuses the full withdrawal; inspect both max views. Reproduce VA-03 with multiple dust legs and no idle USDC. Exercise exclusion/recovery with partial venue liquidity, `totalAssets` reverting, repeated force-remove/re-add, prior holder exit, and zero supply. Confirm deployed proxy implementations, adapter code hashes, venue configuration, and actual caps before any release verdict. No new Foundry test completed for this pass, and no deployed bytecode was inspected.
